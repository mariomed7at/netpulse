# NetPulse Community Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in the NetPulse community a respectful experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include being respectful, giving and receiving constructive feedback gracefully, focusing on what is best for the community, and showing empathy.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insults, discriminatory language, publishing private information without permission, and sustained disruption of community discussions.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit, or reject contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through the contact method listed in the repository profile. Do not post sensitive details in public issues.
